Privacy policy

F&R Check-ins · November 2026

What this site is

F&R Check-ins is the booking page the CUS Fashion & Retail Club internal team uses to schedule 15-minute check-ins with club executives.

What we collect

  • Each executive’s name, role and email address, which the internal team adds ahead of time.
  • The time an executive books and which internal team members will attend.

How we use it

We only use this information to send Google Calendar invites for check-ins and to track who still needs to book. The site connects to the internal team’s Google Calendar only to create, update and cancel those check-in events. It doesn’t read any other calendar data.

Who can see it

Only the F&R internal team can see bookings. We don’t sell or share this information with anyone outside the club, and we delete it once the check-in period ends.
